PixelPDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Introduction

PixelPDF is an image-to-PDF converter designed to help users convert images into PDF documents.

Photos and Files

Images selected for conversion are processed locally in your browser. PixelPDF does not upload your selected images to a server operated by us. Your files are used to generate the PDF you request.

Internet and Third-Party Services

The website may load the jsPDF library from a third-party content delivery network (CDN). That provider may receive technical information such as your IP address when the library is requested. Please review the provider's privacy practices.

Data Collection

We do not operate a server to collect or store the images you select through the website. Your browser or device may retain files you choose to save.

Children's Privacy

PixelPDF does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Please use your device's normal security features and only select files you intend to convert.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its features change.
